Signal logic

Trend + momentum + risk controls

LONG ONLY
  • BUY WATCH: EMA 9 crosses above EMA 21, price above EMA 50, RSI 50–70.
  • SELL / EXIT WATCH: EMA 9 crosses below EMA 21, or price falls below EMA 50.
  • HOLD / WAIT: mixed conditions, overbought momentum, or insufficient / stale data.
  • Uses closed candles for crossover checks to reduce intrabar flicker; this is a rules-based signal engine, not a predictive AI model.
